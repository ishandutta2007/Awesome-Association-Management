# Awesome-Association-Management

## Top Association Management Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Membership CRM, Dues & Renewals, Events, Community Engagement & Nonprofit Association Software*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Association Management**. These systems help professional associations, clubs, and nonprofits manage members, dues, events, directories, and engagement from a single platform.



**Examples** include WildApricot, MemberClicks, GrowthZone, Higher Logic, Mighty Networks, Glue Up, YourMembership, ClubExpress, MemberNova, and Neon CRM (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Commercial AMS products dominate the market. The primary open alternative is **CiviCRM**, with additional tools like **Socius** and CMS membership plugins. This section lists every significant relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[WildApricot, MemberClicks, GrowthZone, YourMembership](https://www.wildapricot.com/)**  

  Established association management systems for memberships, events, websites, and dues collection.



- **[Higher Logic, Mighty Networks](https://www.higherlogic.com/)**  

  Community and engagement platforms often paired with AMS for forums, learning, and member networking.



- **[Glue Up, ClubExpress, MemberNova, Neon CRM](https://www.glueup.com/)**  

  Modern membership and nonprofit CRM tools covering events, payments, and member lifecycle.



- **[Other commercial association platforms](https://www.wildapricot.com/)**  

  Additional AMS and chapter-management solutions for trade associations and clubs.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[CiviCRM](https://github.com/civicrm/civicrm-core)**  

  Leading open-source (AGPL) constituent and membership CRM for nonprofits and associations—contacts, memberships, contributions, events, and case management; runs standalone or with Drupal, WordPress, Joomla, Backdrop.



- **[CiviCRM membership extensions (e.g. Project60)](https://github.com/Project60/org.project60.membership)**  

  Open extensions enhancing membership fee tracking, auto-renewal logic, and European-style membership concepts.



- **[Socius](https://github.com/rufusinfabula/socius)**  

  Open-source association/NGO management—member registry, renewals, payments, events, assemblies, and minutes; self-hosted PHP/MySQL.



- **[Odoo Membership / Nonprofit apps](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Open ERP modules adaptable for membership organizations, dues, and event registration.



- **[WordPress / Drupal membership plugins](https://github.com/search?q=wordpress+membership+OR+drupal+membership+open+source)**  

  Community plugins for paid memberships, directories, and member portals on popular CMS platforms.



- **[Nextcloud / groupware for associations](https://github.com/nextcloud/server)**  

  Open collaboration suites sometimes used for member file sharing, calendars, and internal communication.



- **[Event and ticketing open tools](https://github.com/search?q=open+source+event+management+membership)**  

  Open event systems that integrate with membership databases for conferences and chapter meetings.



- **[Payment & recurring billing open connectors](https://github.com/search?q=recurring+membership+payment+open+source)**  

  Libraries and plugins for dues automation with Stripe, PayPal, and similar processors.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Full AMS alternative**: CiviCRM as the primary open membership and nonprofit CRM.

- **Lightweight associations**: Socius for simpler member registry and renewals.

- **CMS-centric**: WordPress/Drupal membership stacks for content-heavy associations.

- **Composable stacks**: CiviCRM + Higher Logic-style community (or open forum) + payment processor.

- Commercial AMS still leads in polished chapter hierarchies, AMS-specific support, and turnkey websites.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

**CiviCRM** is the standard open association/membership platform.  

**Socius** and CMS plugins suit smaller organizations.  

Commercial AMS (WildApricot, GrowthZone, MemberClicks, Neon, Glue Up, etc.) provide faster setup and dedicated support.  

Many nonprofits run CiviCRM successfully for decades; associations needing heavy chapter automation often choose commercial AMS. Fully open stacks are production-viable with volunteer or partner technical support.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Membership systems process personal and payment data. Configure privacy, consent, and PCI-compliant payments appropriately. Open-source software requires you to manage security updates and backups.

- Commercial platforms shift hosting and support to the vendor. Neither replaces sound membership policies, financial controls, and volunteer/staff processes.
